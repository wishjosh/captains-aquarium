Local derivative, revised 2026-10-13 after silhouette feedback:
dist/stripey-geometry.js rebuilds the body, eyes and fins as code-generated geometry.
dist/fish.js reuses a clone of the source skeleton and Swimming_Normal animation,
and assigns new normalized skin weights to the new geometry. The source GLB,
its original skin weights and all six source clips remain untouched.
The first variant only shortened the snout and changed thickness, eyes and stripes;
that silhouette was rejected by the user and is superseded by the rebuilt surface.

Cute detail revision, 2026-10-13: the user approved the rebuilt silhouette and
requested larger eyes and continued Cute Fish Pack styling. The body and fin
outline are unchanged; code-generated eye lenses, golden rims, highlights,
subtle faceted shading and a few tail fin rays restore the cute detail language.

2026-10-13: Chromis notata uses an independently authored simplified surface in chromis-geometry.js, retaining the same CC0 source skeleton and swimming clip. No external reference photographs or illustrations are embedded.

CURRENT USAGE — 2026-10-16 source-geometry policy supersedes the custom-surface revisions above.
