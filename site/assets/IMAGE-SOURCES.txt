Generated illustrations used by this game
Prepared: 2026-10-13

stripey-flat.png and moon-jelly-flat.png were generated and edited with
OpenAI Imagegen for this game. They are simplified stylized illustrations.
The earlier stripey.png and moon-jelly.png are retained in the local source
but are excluded from the public runtime package because they are unused.

No reference photographs from the species research are embedded in these PNGs.
The Quaternius CC0 declarations and the Three.js MIT license do not grant a
license to these generated illustrations. No separate redistribution license
has been selected for them in this project.
