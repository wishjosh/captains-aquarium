Adaptation for Captain's Aquarium, 2026-10-16:
Runtime jelly.js subdivides and reshapes the original bell into a smooth dome,
with independent contracted/expanded vertex and normal targets. Original thin
tentacles are shortened and repeated around the moving rim. The original central
appendage is replaced with four authored ruffled ribbon surfaces, using its
length as a starting envelope. Four thin horseshoe gonad markings open inward.
Pale translucent materials replace the orange texture. The swim cycle has a
quick contraction, slower relaxation and coast, with delayed oral-arm motion.
The cycle timing and dimensions are stylized game parameters, not measurements.
The adapted 3D asset (geometry/materials/animation) is made available under
CC BY 3.0, with the above attribution; this does not license the entire game.
Species features are stylized for recognition, not a scientific reconstruction.
